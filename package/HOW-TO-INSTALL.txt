BIONIC VOICE - talk to Bionic with a hotkey and hear its answers in a local voice
==================================================================================

What you need
  - Windows 10 or 11 with Bionic (by LM Studio) installed.
  - A microphone. Everything runs on your own computer; nothing is sent online.

Install
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, e.g. Documents\BionicVoice.
     (Keep everything together: BionicVoice.exe needs the _internal and models folders.)
  2. Double-click BionicVoice.exe.
     Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed:
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Set up Bionic once
  3. In Bionic, pick a model in the chat box ("Choose a model").
  4. Click the microphone button in Bionic's chat box and set up voice transcription
     (pick a voice model). Bionic does the speech-to-text itself.

Use it
  A slim panel docks to the side of Bionic's window.
    Hold F8   talk while holding, let go to send
    Tap F8    talk hands-free; it sends after a short silence or when you tap again
    F7        misspoke? clear it and try again (nothing is sent)
    F9        stop reading the answer aloud
    Shift+F9  read the last answer again
  Hover over any panel button to see what it does. Voices, speed, auto-stop time and
  the hotkeys can all be changed from the panel (Voice, Speed, Setup buttons).

Start together with Bionic
  Click Setup on the panel and tick "Start automatically with Windows".
  Bionic Voice then starts when you sign in, stays invisible, and appears next to
  Bionic whenever you open it. Untick it to turn that off.

Uninstall
  Untick "Start automatically with Windows", click Quit on the panel, delete the folder.

If something doesn't work
  - "Bionic's chat box isn't on screen": open a chat in Bionic (not Settings).
  - "Voice transcription isn't set up": do step 4 above.
  - Your antivirus might warn about the global hotkeys (the app listens for F7/F8/F9
    to work while Bionic is in the background). That is all it uses the keyboard for;
    the full source code is in the "source" folder.
  - Details of any error are in overlay.log next to BionicVoice.exe.
